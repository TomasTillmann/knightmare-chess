# Coup on Crab: source review

Conclusion: the strongest source-supported interpretation is that Coup played after Crab overrides Crab's movement and gives the replacement King ordinary Pawn movement. This is an inference from an express PALADIN ruling plus the conflict rule; the reviewed sources contain no express COUP-on-CRAB ruling.

- `cards.md`, Crab: a transformed Pawn moves and captures one square diagonally forward and retains promotion. Coup: the replacement King keeps its “standard move.” Those card texts alone leave the meaning of standard ambiguous for a transformed piece.
- `rules.md` §10 and §15.3: transformations retain an original identity; Coup's replacement retains ordinary movement. These sections do not explicitly resolve the Crab interaction.
- `official-faq.pdf`, printed page 20: the specific sequence PALADIN → COUP expressly makes the movement effects conflict. The later Coup wins, and the new King moves as the underlying Knight. This directly establishes that Coup can override a prior transformation's movement; it does not mention Crab.
- `official-faq.pdf`, printed page 21: an active Crab retains promotion but loses the Pawn's other special movement abilities, including its initial double move and en passant. This describes Crab movement; it does not address Coup.
- `official-rulebook.pdf`, Conflicts: when both conflicting cards are Continuing Effects, the last card played takes precedence. Crab is listed as a Continuing Effect in `rules.md`; the Coup FAQ expressly identifies Coup as one.

Applying the PALADIN ruling to the corresponding Crab movement conflict therefore favors ordinary Pawn movement under the later Coup. Retaining Crab diagonals would require treating Crab differently from PALADIN despite the same Coup wording. Report this as a well-supported analogy, not a quoted official Crab-specific ruling. The evidence establishes the movement interpretation; it does not separately establish every consequence of suspending Crab's transformation or other attributes.

Scope: source review only; no engine or test sources inspected, and no probes executed.
