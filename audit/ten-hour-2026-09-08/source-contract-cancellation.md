# Cancellation source comparison

Source-only review; observed engine behavior is supplied by the parent and was not independently inspected.

Sources read: `cards.md` Confabulation, Peace Talks, and Coup entries; `rules.md` §§15.3–15.4 and 22.6; publisher `official-faq.pdf` printed/PDF pages 18–19 and 50. No engine or test sources inspected.

| Supplied observation | Explicit local mismatch | Local omission or ambiguity | Publisher comparison |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canceling Confabulation leaves one component away. | Not established from these cancellation clauses alone. Peace Talks requires immediate cancellation of the effect, but neither local entry defines component placement after cancellation. | §15.4 expressly calls its component guidance recommended and provisional; it does not define cancellation placement, return timing, or the meaning of an away component. The general next-move correction rule does not identify how to separate components. | Page 18 describes Confabulation being undone, earlier component effects resuming their original scope, and effects placed on the composite losing their target. This supports restoring component identity, but the permitted pages do not specify the components' squares or zones. An away component alone is therefore insufficient to establish a publisher placement mismatch from this bounded source set. |
| Canceling a royal Confabulation leaves its dependent Coup active instead of restoring the Prince's royalty. | No explicit local Confabulation-to-Coup cancellation clause. The general immediate-cancellation text alone does not state which dependent cards expire. | Local §15.3 explains royal replacement and direct Coup cancellation; §15.4 omits the dependent-Coup rule. | Definite mismatch: pages 19 and 50 expressly require Coup to end when Confabulation ends, restoring the Prince as King. |
| Peace Talks may cancel that royal Confabulation after its Prince has been captured. | If Coup itself is canceled and the player is left without a King, this directly violates §§15.3 and 22.6. For cancellation targeting Confabulation while incorrectly retaining Coup, the specific prohibition is not explicit locally. | The missing dependent-Coup rule conceals why canceling Confabulation would remove the remaining royalty. Importing that publisher rule makes the local King-loss prohibition applicable. | Definite mismatch: pages 19 and 50 explicitly make both Confabulation and Coup immune to Peace Talks after the Prince has been captured. |

The two royal cases are precise publisher-contract failures with a missing local cross-card rule. Treat the away-component case as unresolved cancellation placement in this bounded comparison; do not promote it to an explicit local or publisher requirement without the relevant separation ruling.
