# Return and transformation source contract

Sources: rules.md §10, §15.4–15.5; cards.md “Resurrection” and “Winged Victory”.

- A transformed piece captured and rescued **during that move or the immediately following move** retains its transformation. A later return restores its original type (§10). “Move” is undefined here: turn, ply, or full move must not be assumed.
- Temporary transformations such as Crab follow that retention cutoff. Original-type effects still apply unless the transformation says otherwise; a transformed Pawn remains a Pawn target (§10).
- Pawn promotion is permanent. Resurrection preserves promotion; a Pawn promoted to Rook, Bishop, or Knight is eligible and uses the promoted type’s starting squares (§15.5).
- Resurrection excludes owned captured pieces that are royal or current/original Kings or Queens, and excludes dead, away, on-board, and opposing-owned pieces. Temporary unpromoted transformations use the original physical type for destination eligibility, independently of whether their powers survive return. Promoted pieces use their promoted type. Either matching owner-relative starting square is valid; vacancy and Forbidden City restrictions apply (§15.5).
- Winged Victory targets an opponent-captured Pawn and places it on an empty central square (cards.md). Under §10, a temporarily transformed original Pawn remains targetable. These excerpts do not explicitly resolve Winged Victory eligibility for permanently promoted Pawns.
- Resurrection returns former composite components separately as original pieces (§15.5). Section 15.4 labels its later-return component rule a recommended ruling requiring official confirmation; it does not specify composite preservation on immediate rescue.
