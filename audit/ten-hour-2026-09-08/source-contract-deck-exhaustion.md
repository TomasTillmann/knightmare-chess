Separate-deck exhaustion contract (source-only)

Rules §§4.1–4.2 and §9 distinguish the standard separate-deck game from the common-deck variant. In the standard game, an empty draw deck is never replenished by reshuffling discards. Players may continue using cards in hand; an empty hand leaves ordinary chess subject to Continuing Effects still in play.

Section 9 requires one immediate replacement draw after playing a card. This replacement is distinct from cards added by a card's own effect. Vulture retrieves the opponent's last played card and, with separate decks, also discards the user's top undrawn card. Legacy retrieves one chosen card from the user's discard pile, or the common discard pile in that variant. Neither card authorizes reshuffling a separate draw deck. Sources do not specify how Vulture's top-undrawn-card requirement behaves when none remains.

Ordinary optional discard is available after one's own turn only when no card was played during that turn: discard one, then draw a replacement. The common-deck variant additionally permits discard/replacement when no card is played on the opponent's turn and explicitly reshuffles its discard pile upon deck exhaustion.
