# Chaos: alternative promotion source review

**Conclusion:** a7–a8=Knight after cancellation of a7–a8=Queen is a different resulting board state. Treating it as an acceptable replacement move is the strongest inference from the FAQ's board-state criterion; the reviewed sources do not expressly rule on changing the promotion choice.

**Express rulings:** `cards.md:289–291` requires a different move and permits using the same piece or another piece; it supplies no promotion-specific restriction. `official-faq.pdf`, printed p. 50 (repeated on pp. 17, 38, and 59), makes the board state the criterion for THINK AGAIN!, KNIGHTMARE!, and CHAOS, disregarding off-board captured/dead assignments. It forbids merely changing an unrelated card play while preserving the board. Printed pp. 17, 38, and 60 reject a different route to the same destination and describe a move as a piece's displacement. Printed p. 59 permits replaying the same two BROTHERS IN ARMS pawns if at least one changes destination. None of those examples discusses promotion.

**Inference and ambiguity:** Queen versus Knight changes the on-board piece type, unlike a changed route or an off-board bookkeeping change, so it satisfies the ordinary reading of the board-state criterion. The displacement wording, taken as an exhaustive identity rule, could instead reject the unchanged a7–a8 displacement. Its actual context only resolves route differences. This tension is an interpretive ambiguity, not an explicit prohibition of alternative promotion, and the FAQ contains no express promotion-cancellation example in the relevant passages. No engine or test source was inspected and no engine behavior is asserted.
