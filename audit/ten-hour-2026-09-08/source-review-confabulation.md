# Confabulation cancellation: publisher FAQ evidence

Scope: source review only. Read official FAQ PDF pages 17–20 and 50; no engine or test inspection. Page numbers below are the printed PDF page numbers.

- **Pre-merge continuing effects (express, p. 18):** A continuing effect already on a constituent applies to the whole merged piece. A continuing effect creating a different named piece (notably Crab) applies only to that constituent. After Peace Talks removes Confabulation, pre-merge effects act as they did before merging; the original target of pre-merge Pacifism remains Pacified.
- **Post-merge continuing effects (express for Pacifism/Neutrality, p. 18):** Pacifism or Neutrality placed on the merged piece affects neither constituent after cancellation. The FAQ explains that the merged target ceases to exist. Generalizing this to every post-merge effect is an inference; these pages expressly address the named examples and separately Coup.
- **Constituent placement (not specified in these pages):** The FAQ describes two pieces merging, the merged target disappearing when undone, and prior effects resuming. It does not expressly require the off-board constituent to return, identify a return square, specify when such a return occurs, resolve occupied squares, or say which constituent remains. Its reference to pre-merge effects resuming does not establish any of those placement mechanics. Treating it as an express restoration/return rule would overstate the source.
- **Dependent Coup (express, pp. 19 and 50):** Coup is legal on a merged piece only if it contains neither a Rook nor a Queen. Ending Confabulation also removes that Coup and restores the Prince to King.
- **Prince captured (express, pp. 19 and 50):** If that Prince has been captured, both Confabulation and Coup are immune to Peace Talks. This expressly preserves them against Peace Talks; these passages do not establish immunity to every possible cancellation mechanism.

Conclusion: the source clearly establishes attachment provenance and the Confabulation–Coup dependency/protection. It does not settle constituent return placement. No claim of engine correctness is made.
