# Revenge timing source review

Sources inspected visually: `final_cards/KC6_card2.png` (Revenge) and `final_cards/KC7_card2.png` (Dark Mirror).

Revenge's printed timing permits play when your Pawn has just been captured without a card. Dark Mirror is itself a card that lets a Pawn capture diagonally backward for that move, and is played instead of the move. The ordinary reading therefore excludes Revenge immediately after Dark Mirror was used to capture your Pawn: that capture used a card.

The artwork does not define “without a card” further. A narrower interpretation distinguishing direct card removal from a card-enabled board capture would need additional authority; neither inspected card states that exception. This review makes no claim about engine behavior.
