# Coup suspension source comparison

Scope: source comparison only, using the supplied observations; no engine or test sources inspected. References to local files use repository line numbers; PDF references use physical PDF pages.

| Supplied behavior | Explicit local contract | Publisher ruling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coup leaves Pacifism active on the new King. | Suspension is required by composition: `rules.md:215` suspends a Continuing Effect when its initial condition fails; `cards.md:43` excludes the King from Pacifism; `rules.md:447` makes the replacement a King subject to King rules. `rules.md:480–484` describes Pacifism's effects but supplies no exception to suspension. The local Coup section does not state this interaction individually. | `official-faq.pdf`, p.20, expressly permits Coup on a Pacifist piece and suspends Pacifism because it cannot apply to a King. Observed continued activity conflicts with that ruling. |
| Coup leaves Neutrality active on the new King. | The same general suspension rule applies (`rules.md:215`); Neutrality excludes Kings and royal pieces (`rules.md:435`; also `cards.md:287`), and Coup confers King status (`rules.md:447`). `rules.md:437` retains the marker through transformations and says promotion/transformation does not itself **end** Neutrality. That wording requires persistence of the effect, but does not explicitly require continuous activity or prohibit temporary suspension. Thus it can be reconciled with suspension; it is not an explicit local override authorizing an active neutral King. | `official-faq.pdf`, p.20, expressly suspends Neutrality on the Coup King. Observed continued activity conflicts with that ruling. |
| Coup stays active after its royal Pawn promotes to Rook or Queen. | `rules.md:305–307` permits normal Pawn promotion to those types; `cards.md:159` excludes Rooks and Queens as Coup replacements. The general rule (`rules.md:215`) consequently requires suspension once that eligibility condition fails. `rules.md:445–449` omits a card-specific promotion/suspension procedure; it does not grant a promotion exception or prohibit promotion. | `official-faq.pdf`, pp.20–21, expressly allows the promotion and requires Coup suspension for Rook or Queen, until the disqualifying type ceases. P.44 repeats the p.21 ruling. Observed continued activity conflicts with all these explicit rulings. |

Local versus publisher status: the local contract already contains the general suspension requirement. The named Coup interactions are derived by applying that explicit general rule to explicit eligibility exclusions, rather than quoted as individually enumerated local rules. The publisher removes any doubt about that application. The publisher rulebook, `official-rulebook.pdf`, p.1, “Continuing Effects,” likewise requires suspension while initial conditions fail and preserves the card for later reactivation.

Publisher follow-through: FAQ pp.21/44 says a promoted Rook resumes previously suspended Pacifism and Neutrality; a promoted Queen resumes only Pacifism. Local eligibility exclusions and `rules.md:215` support this distinction. Local text does not individually describe those resumptions, the exact restoration of the former King's royal status during Coup suspension, or what to do if that Prince is already lost. `rules.md:449` only addresses Peace Talks cancellation after loss of the Prince; it must not silently be treated as a complete suspension procedure.

Conclusion: all three supplied active-effect behaviors conflict with the publisher's explicit rulings and with the local general suspension rule applied to the corresponding target restrictions. Neutrality's preservation sentence is compatible with suspension and is not evidence of an intentional local departure. No claim is made here about engine implementation details.
