# Cancellation of an additional move: source contract

The requirements coexist: restore the state before the canceled additional movement, retain the completed first movement and its clocks, and reopen only the canceled movement's replacement opportunity.

- **Position and opportunity — explicit locally.** Chaos cancels the latest movement, expressly including an additional-card movement. Restore its immediate predecessor position, consequences, clocks, and pending obligations; preserve independent earlier moves and keep the moving player active for a different replacement. Thus the first Regular Move survives cancellation of the second movement. The replacement can use the same or another piece, subject to legality and the physical-repeat prohibition. ([rules.md:508](/tmp/knightmare-engine-audit-20260909/rules.md:508), [rules.md:512–516](/tmp/knightmare-engine-audit-20260909/rules.md:512))
- **Completion and clocks — explicit locally for Crusade/Merciless.** Their extra movement leaves the already completed Regular Move intact, grants no third ordinary move, and does not increment halfmove or advance fullmove a second time; capture/Pawn resets still apply. The Merciless paragraph is line **696**, not 698. ([rules.md:688](/tmp/knightmare-engine-audit-20260909/rules.md:688), [rules.md:696](/tmp/knightmare-engine-audit-20260909/rules.md:696))
- **Replacing the canceled extra through an ordinary-move API — inference, not an explicit API rule.** That action fills the canceled extra movement's slot within the existing turn. Consistent application of the preceding local requirements means retaining the first move's clock advancement and avoiding a second fullmove advancement/halfmove increment, while applying any replacement capture/Pawn reset. An API's name cannot itself make the replacement a new Regular Move or erase the surviving first one. The source does not separately spell out this exact cancellation-plus-ordinary-API clock case; this is the coherent derived ruling, not a publisher quotation.
- **FEN side-to-move — representation convention only.** None of the inspected sources mentions FEN or `turn.color`. They therefore do **not** require those two fields to match. The actor must actually have a usable replacement opportunity. Whether FEN is normalized to that actor or retains ordinary-chess side-to-move while variant state supplies the actor is an engine design choice. A mismatch alone is not source-grounded proof of a defect; denying the actor legal replacement actions would violate the explicit local contract.

Publisher support and limits: [Official Rulings, pp. 16–18 and 37–38](https://www.sjgames.com/knightmare/KnightmareChess_FAQ.pdf) requires a different physical displacement, permits taking back the responsible card, and preserves the other replacement movement under Plots Within Plots. Page 18 treats Charge, Merciless, and Crusade as two piece movements within one overall Move. These pages establish neither FEN semantics nor halfmove/fullmove handling, and do not expressly decide cancellation of only the second movement of those three cards; the local latest-movement rule supplies that precision. The explicit extra-clock clauses inspected cover Crusade and Merciless; extending the same clock policy to Charge is supported by their shared publisher classification but remains an inference within this review's narrow source scope.
