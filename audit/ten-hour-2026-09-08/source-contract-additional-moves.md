# Additional-move source contract

- **Same physical piece:** Charge! permits one Knight two consecutive moves (`cards.md:155`); Crusade says “This Bishop” (`cards.md:263`); Merciless says “The Rook” (`cards.md:279`). These descriptions identify the same piece. Physical identity and rejection of replacement occupants are explicit for Crusade in `rules.md:684`, and Merciless explicitly requires the same physical Rook in `rules.md:692`.
- **Quiet first, potentially capturing second:** all three card descriptions expressly prohibit capture on the first move. They impose no second-move capture prohibition, so permission there is inferred from ordinary movement in the card descriptions. It is explicit for Crusade (`rules.md:686`) and Merciless (`rules.md:696`): the additional move may capture, subject to ordinary restrictions.
- **Castling Rook and Merciless:** the card paragraph alone leaves whether “move a Rook” includes castling unstated. `rules.md:694` explicitly permits the relocated castling Rook, requires its post-castling square, leaves the King in place, and excludes an unmoved second Rook.
- **Clocks and turn:** card descriptions call these immediate additional moves but do not specify clocks. Crusade (`rules.md:688`) and Merciless (`rules.md:696`) explicitly leave the turn after its completed Regular Move, grant no third ordinary move, and forbid an additional fullmove advance or halfmove increment. Captures or the specified Pawn component reset the halfmove clock. The supplied rule excerpt establishes no explicit Charge! clock rule.
