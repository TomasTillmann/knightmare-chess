# Toll checking reaction and Haunting Memories copy contract

## Verified contract

- **Explicit Toll rule:** cards.md 89–91 requires the opponent to have just crossed the frontier toward the reactor. The mover chooses a Pawn payment or declines, in which case the turn is canceled, any card used is returned, and another move is forbidden. Publisher FAQ printed p.41 explicitly retains Toll eligibility after an outward crossing followed by a return using Charge, Merciless, or Crusade. A final square back on the original side does not erase the crossing.
- **Explicit check restriction:** publisher FAQ printed p.4 answers a Bog reaction that leaves the mover's King in check: a regular card cannot cause either player to place or leave that player's own King in check at the end of that player's own turn. Such a card has no effect, but is spent and replaced. This wording covers the turn owner even when the other player played the reaction. It is stronger and less ambiguous than reading only the local phrase “acting King” in rules.md 269–273.
- **Nonmating check is not blanket permission:** rules.md 242–246 permits regular-card check, while rules.md 267–275 imposes end-of-own-turn safety. FAQ p.4's Riposte example specifically explains why the restored piece does not end the mover's turn in check: the opponent loses the next turn and cannot capture then. It is not permission to leave an otherwise threatening piece checking the mover after a completed move.
- **Next-turn obligations:** FAQ p.3 allows an apparently mated player to escape on that player's own turn. That rule does not authorize a reaction to leave the current mover illegally checked at the end of the current turn. If Toll payment newly exposes the mover's King after the completed move and the resulting turn would end in check, the general p.4 rule requires the regular card to have no effect. Applying that general rule to Toll is an inference from the explicit universal rule, not a Toll-specific FAQ example. The reviewed sources do not prescribe an engine API's exact pending-payment states or retry behavior.
- **Explicit Haunting Memories copy rule:** cards.md 167–169 and rules.md 750–754 specify the same effect as the last played card, disallowing duplication of a unique card from the copier's own deck while allowing an opponent's unique card. FAQ p.34 explicitly confirms a Haunting Memories copy chain preserves the underlying Peace Talks effect, and its Heir example preserves the unique-card restriction through copying. Local rules.md 754 labels latest-successfully-declared-card selection and copying text/classification as a recommended ruling.
- **Toll copy implication:** an otherwise eligible Haunting Memories copying Toll must apply Toll's crossing and payment/cancellation effect and the general check restrictions. Copying supplies no express exemption from timing, legal targets, or own-turn King safety. The reviewed pages contain no Toll–Haunting Memories pair-specific ruling; exact reaction-window eligibility should be distinguished from this copied-effect inference.

Sources: local `cards.md` 89–91, 167–169; `rules.md` 228–275 and 750–754; [publisher official FAQ](https://www.sjgames.com/knightmare/KnightmareChess_FAQ.pdf), printed pp.3–4, 33–34, 41. Only those source materials were read; no engine or test sources were consulted. The selected text sections were sufficient, so artwork inspection was unnecessary.
