# Exhausted escape-card allowance

Reviewed local `rules.md` §§8.5, 11.5–11.6, publisher FAQ pp.3–5, and rulebook card-play/Checkmate sections. No engine or test sources inspected.

**Conclusion:** Once an otherwise valid, unrelated Pacifism has resolved and consumed the checked player's sole card allowance, and no ordinary move or other permitted continuation can cure check, the game must end in checkmate. Hidden Passage remaining in hand is no longer an available escape. This conclusion combines explicit general rules; neither source gives this exact voluntary-Pacifism example.

**Explicit support:** Local §8.5 permits one own-turn card and counts even ineffective cards as played. Section 11.5 ends the game when the checked player lacks any available legal escape sequence. Publisher FAQ p.3 expressly grants the apparently mated player an escape turn, but also says that cancellation of the only saving card by Fog of War leaves both allowances spent and the defender checkmated if no legal move remains. Thus possession of an escape card does not suffice after its legal opportunity has disappeared.

**Fizzle distinction:** FAQ p.4 and local §11.6 address regular cards causing an illegal final self-check, and §11.6 expressly restores a regular card played after the Regular Move when it leaves the acting King checked. Pacifism is a Continuing Effect played before moving in this scenario. Neither cited passage explicitly mandates reversing it simply because the player voluntarily spent their last escape resource. The rulebook expressly allows Continuing Effects to cause or assist mate. Even applying a fizzle interpretation would normally still spend the allowance, leaving the same unavoidable mate.

**Limits:** Sources do not prescribe the software event at which this must be announced. Immediate adjudication once no legal continuation exists follows §11.5, but rejecting the voluntary declaration before commitment is not explicitly addressed. A permanently live checked position with no possible legal completion is unsupported. Recheck actual legal resources after Pacifism: it can suppress an attack if its target matters, and exceptions such as previously established Plots Within Plots change the premise.
