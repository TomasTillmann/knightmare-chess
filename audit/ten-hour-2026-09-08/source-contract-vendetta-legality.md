# Vendetta legality source contract

Sources: `cards.md` Vendetta; `rules.md` 554–559 and 281–293.

- **Trigger — explicit:** playing Vendetta makes each player capture with their move if able. No additional activation or timing restriction appears here.
- **Duration and expiry — explicit:** the effect continues until the current player has no legal capture. One player’s inability to capture ends it; it does not merely suspend that player’s obligation.
- **Legality — explicit:** a pseudo-capture leaving the player’s King in check is illegal and cannot keep Vendetta active. Therefore a pinned piece’s apparent capture counts only if that particular capture legally preserves King safety; being pinned does not categorically disqualify every capture.
- **Card assistance — explicit:** a player need not play a card merely because it could enable a capture. **Implication:** hypothetical card-enabled captures alone cannot prevent expiry when ordinary legal captures are absent.
- **Move choice — explicit obligation, direct implication:** when a legal capture exists, an ordinary noncapturing move is unavailable; the player must capture.
- **Conflicts — explicit:** Checkmate Rule has highest priority; explicit card text overrides ordinary chess. Resolve conflicts narrowly, preserving unrelated restrictions. Vendetta supplies no permission to ignore King safety or other capture-legality constraints.
