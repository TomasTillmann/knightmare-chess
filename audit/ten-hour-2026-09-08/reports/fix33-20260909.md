## Source verdict

Inspected `cards.md`, `rules.md` §§15.3, 15.4, 16.1, 16.3, 19.1, original artwork `KC11_card4.png` (Man-Trap), `KC10_card4.png` (Coup), `KC3_card3.png` (Pacifism), and publisher FAQ PDF pages 40 and 46. The existing source review `source-contract-man-trap-royal.md` agrees with the relevant ruling.

The publisher explicitly states that Kings and Pacifists do not set off Man-Trap. That clarification supersedes the printed King-springs-but-is-unaffected wording; it rules out both trap consumption and order-dependent Pacifist capture. Coup makes the marked Knight, Bishop, or Pawn the current King, so its protection follows by composition with Coup's text and §15.3. Confabulation carries component protection under §15.4. A neutral entrant is capturable; a newly arriving Mystic Shield recipient is captured before protection begins on the following turn.

Source: https://www.sjgames.com/knightmare/KnightmareChess_FAQ.pdf (live fetch returned HTTP 403; inspected the existing local `official-faq.pdf` directly with `pdftotext -f 40 -l 40` and `-f 46 -l 46`). Extracted pages are saved in `/tmp/knightmare-fix33-faq40.txt` and `/tmp/knightmare-fix33-faq46.txt`.

`cards.md` remains a transcription of the artwork. The engine catalog description and local §19.1 now state the publisher clarification.
