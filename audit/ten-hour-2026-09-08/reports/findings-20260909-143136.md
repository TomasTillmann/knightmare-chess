## Rejected or qualified hypotheses

- Cancellation of an additional move restores the pre-extra FEN while reopening the original actor's replacement opportunity. This can make FEN's side field differ from turn.color beforeMove. Both stopped short-deck seeds9098015/9098035 replayed identically, and a36group/180action reduction continued legally in all cases. No inspected source defines FEN equality in this state; the mismatch alone is excluded from the bug count. Replacing the canceled extra through an ordinary move increments the halfmove clock again and, for Black, advances the fullmove number again; avoiding that is a coherent derived rule, not an explicit source/API convention. Recorded as qualified in `chaos-additional-turn-observation.ts` and `source-contract-chaos-additional-turn.md`.

- Vulture with no undrawn card is accepted and retrieves the opponent's physical card without a cost card. Source text requires discarding the top undrawn card but does not settle an empty-deck case; this remains qualified. One-/two-card deck controls correctly distinguish the cost from the normal replacement draw (`retrieval-empty-deck-domain.ts`, `source-contract-deck-exhaustion.md`).

- First-rank Annexation permits its two-square advance but creates no en-passant rights. Second-rank Annexation correctly grants both simultaneous capture opportunities. Guardian without a follower likewise creates no en-passant right; its optional Knight/King follower and King-safety rollback work. The card-specific wording and general first-/second-rank en-passant rule do not explicitly settle these exact combinations, so they remain qualified observations, not unambiguous defects (`annexation-guardian-ep-domain.ts`, `source-contract-annexation-first-rank.md`, `source-contract-guardian-follower.md`). Guardian first-rank double-step rejection is likewise qualified.

- Truce/Vendetta priority: not a defect. FAQ61/63 explicitly discards Vendetta regardless of play order.
- Mystic Shield classification: it is NOT a Continuing Effect, as confirmed by the complete sentence on FAQ5. A truncated text search initially looked ambiguous; classification is correct.
- Panic timing while checked remains a qualified rules question, not a confirmed finding.
- Bog after Ghostwalk correctly stops the Rook on the first empty square after one or two friendly blockers (FAQ13). The two direct probes pass; the suspected fixed-one-square bug is rejected.
- Peace Talks correctly fizzles both self-check from removing Pacifism and direct mate from removing Neutrality; the universal transition guard handles these, despite no local guard in the card handler. See `peace-talks-safety-finding.ts`(two successful safety probes).
- Fortification geometry remains qualified: FAQ30–31 diagrams describe walls spanning multiple square boundaries, while local artwork KC13_card4 describes a wall between two adjacent squares. The rendered diagram and artwork were inspected; this source discrepancy is not yet classified as an engine defect.
- Masquerade rejects a Knight obtained by ordinary Pawn promotion while accepting an original Knight (`masquerade-promotion-finding.ts`). Independent source-only review found that the rulebook scopes retained original targeting to Continuing Effect transformations, but FAQ7 discusses promoted Pawns primarily as physical-piece accounting. The interpretation that Masquerade should accept the promoted Knight is plausible; no explicit ruling was found. Moved from confirmed to qualified; see `source-review-promotion.md`.
- Chaos, Knightmare, and Think Again reject redoing a canceled Queen promotion as Rook/Bishop/Knight on the same destination. All three reject an identical Queen promotion and permit a different destination in controls (`chaos-promotion-finding.ts`,12promotion cases+3controls). FAQ50's board-state criterion strongly supports allowing the changed on-board role, but FAQ17 defines a move using displacement when discussing alternate routes. No explicit promotion ruling was found; independent source review retains this as qualified (`source-review-chaos-promotion.md`).

Additional qualified source questions: Earthquake's physical clockwise direction requires a clarified coordinate contract. With coordinates attached to the board and stationary players, clockwise rotation makes White advance toward decreasing files; with coordinates fixed to the table, pieces rotate and White still advances toward increasing ranks. The current engine keeps piece coordinates and uses increasing files after clockwise rotation. Repository wording mixes the two conventions; this is qualified pending an explicit representation decision (`source-review-earthquake-coordinates.md`). Rotated Pawn/Crab/en-passant domain checks validate the engine's chosen orientation contract, not the physical clockwise naming.

Standalone Crab using Dark Mirror is not expressly settled in the inspected sources. The rulebook permits original-type targeting but gives Continuing Effects precedence in conflicts. The all-Crab Dark Mirror rejection in the composite harness is recorded as an observation, not a conformance control (`source-review-crab-movement.md`).

Coup/Crab order is also qualified. A royal Pawn without Crab moves straight; with Crab it moves diagonally without capture in either play order (`coup-crab-order-observation.ts`,three groups,14.6ms). FAQ20's earlier Paladin/later Coup example suggests Coup should restore normal movement, but no explicit Crab/Coup ruling was found (`source-review-coup-crab-movement.md`).
