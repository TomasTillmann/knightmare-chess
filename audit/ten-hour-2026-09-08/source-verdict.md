Therefore `returnCard:false` on an optional nonmovement card retains its effects, physical card disposition, draw, and spent allowance. Default/true retrieves the card, removes its effects, reverses its draw, and refunds its allowance. This is a source-grounded interpretation of that distinction; the FAQ does not name this engine payload. Continuing Effects retained this way follow physical identities; Crab on the moved Pawn follows it back, and Coup retains its royal attributes. FAQ pages 17/38/50 prohibit repeating the board move merely by changing or omitting a card, taking a different route, or changing off-board captured/dead status. The Plots ruling limits cancellation to the involved move; independent earlier moves/cards and response allowances survive. Rulebook turn sequence, physical replacement draws, Continuing Effects, optional discarding, and the regular-card Checkmate Rule remain applicable.
