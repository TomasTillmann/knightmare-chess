# L1 adjudication: Curse versus Anathema/Siege

**Decision: unresolved/inconsistent local rule text, not a confirmed engine bug. Do not change the engine to permit long cursed-piece swaps on this evidence.**

**Official evidence.** The [publisher FAQ](https://www.sjgames.com/knightmare/KnightmareChess_FAQ.pdf), page 40, MEDUSA answer, explicitly states “Swapping is considered a move”. It explains that MEDUSA generally prevents swapping because its Continuing Effect takes precedence over non-Continuing Effects. This is an explicit swap ruling in MEDUSA’s context; applying it to Curse is an inference, not a Curse-specific adjudication. No Curse-specific answer was identified in the supplied evidence.

**Card evidence.** [cards.md](../../cards.md), lines 161–163, limits the cursed piece to one or two squares. Anathema (93–95) and Siege (225–227) specify exchanges without an explicit Curse exemption. Together with the FAQ, this supports retaining the rejection, although it does not conclusively settle Curse.

**Opposing local evidence.** [rules.md](../../rules.md) §20:646 expressly calls Anathema a non-move. Siege (§20:660, referring to 644) ignores movement geometry but does not expressly exempt Curse. Section 6:118 waives ordinary move availability; §6:80 defines displacement as movement. Section 1:7–16 mixes official material with local interpretations and acknowledges missing card metadata. The non-move reading therefore cannot establish an official exemption.

**Disposition.** [README.md:28](README.md) correctly qualifies L1. [game-026.md:21,39](game-026.md) and [game-060.md:21,42](game-060.md) document rejection; game 026’s “confirmed” conclusion overstates authority. Keep L1 qualified pending authoritative clarification or an explicit house ruling. No production change is justified under the real-bugs-only goal; this decision covers Anathema/Siege only.
