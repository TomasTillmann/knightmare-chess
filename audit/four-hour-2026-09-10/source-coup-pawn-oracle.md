# One-Coup royal-Pawn oracle: source scope

Source-only review, 2026-09-10. No production, test, or executable harness sources were inspected.

The local contract in `rules.md` §15.3 expressly says a royal Pawn promotes normally: Bishop/Knight promotion leaves Coup active, while Queen/Rook promotion suspends Coup and restores the previous King through remaining active Coups. With exactly one Coup for that owner and the original Prince still on the board, the independent expected state is therefore unambiguous: B/N leaves the promoted physical Pawn royal; Q/R makes it nonroyal and restores royalty to the original Prince. The physical Coup card remains beside the board. Its suspension is not cancellation.

The printed Coup text in `cards.md` makes the original King a capturable Prince and transfers King protection to the marked piece while preserving its ordinary movement. The official FAQ's royal-Pawn promotion answer expressly permits every ordinary promotion role and says Q/R suspends Coup. Its Peace Talks answer separately prohibits canceling Coup after the Prince is lost if that would leave no King.

King safety must be evaluated on the fully resolved resulting royal position, rather than always on the promoting Pawn. Thus B/N requires the promoted Pawn's square to be safe; Q/R with a surviving Prince requires that Prince's square to be safe. This is a direct application of the local transfer-of-royalty and final King-safety rules. The safety assertion is for a completed turn: §11.6 permits temporary check when an available same-turn card rescues the resulting royal. A move-only oracle should remove those card resources or model that rescue separately.

Excluded ambiguity: with the original Prince already captured and no other active Coup, Q/R promotion would suspend the only royal marker without an on-board King to restore. The reviewed promotion answers do not expressly prescribe rejection, surrender, or another outcome for that exact combination. The Peace Talks ruling concerns a regular cancellation card and cannot by itself settle an ordinary promotion. Exclude that configuration from a correctness oracle unless the project adopts an explicit rule; do not invent an expected legality or count an engine disagreement as a confirmed defect.

Exact source locations: frozen `rules.md` lines 176–186 (promotion before final King-safety verification), 272–282 (temporary check and rescue), 309–317 (normal promotion), 453–463 (Coup); frozen `cards.md` lines 157–159; official FAQ text lines 184–188 (Peace Talks after lost Prince), 837–840 (Q/R suspension, printed page 20), 883–889 and 1874–1880 (all promotion roles, printed pages 21 and 44). These sources do not provide an explicit answer to the excluded captured-Prince promotion combination. The opposite-color neutral Coup surrender example addresses a different mechanism and does not settle it either.
