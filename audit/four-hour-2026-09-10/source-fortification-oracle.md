# Fortification movement-oracle source scope

Source-only review; no engine or test sources inspected. The proposed ordinary-movement oracle is supported by the repository contract in `rules.md` §14.4 (lines 421–431). The source layers must remain distinct: the exact graph-edge representation is the local precision, whereas the card and publisher FAQ establish walls, jumping exceptions, and distinct wall obstructions.

`cards.md` lines 205–207 describes a wall between directly or diagonally neighboring squares and permits Knight-style jumps or explicit card jumps to cross it. `rules.md` lines 423–427 specifies an undirected adjacent-square boundary, permits occupied endpoints, and says a wall neither occupies an endpoint nor blocks a different approach boundary. Thus a diagonal edge blocks only its chosen adjacent pair under this engine contract; it does not create unrelated blocked corner crossings.

`rules.md` line 431 explicitly applies the boundary test to every adjacent step of a slider or multi-square Pawn move, and to captures, en passant, and checks. Ordinary non-Knight attack geometry therefore receives the same wall filter as movement; Knight jumps retain their exemption. These details are explicit local contract, not claimed as verbatim publisher answers.

Publisher FAQ text lines 536–537 (page 13; repeated lines 1370–1371, page 31) treats a wall edge and a neighboring piece or Forbidden City as separate obstructions. The diagram and question at lines 1329–1341 (page 30), answered at lines 1349–1351 (page 31), allow three listed diagonal approaches while blocking the wall-crossing alternatives; an adjacent Forbidden City does not close the clear vertices. Lines 1383–1389 (page 31) describe two adjacent Fortifications remaining distinct and retaining a diagonal passage between them. This supports avoiding automatic wall fusion. The older FAQ's examples describe walls separating more than one pair; they do not independently prove that its physical geometry is identical to this repository's single-pair graph representation.

Only line 429 of the local section is explicitly marked a recommended ruling: separate checks for multi-segment moves and the absence of an implicit Ghostwalk wall exemption. Both are outside this oracle's chosen scope. The graph-edge representation and ordinary movement/capture/check refinements at lines 423–427 and 431 are explicit local requirements, although their exact API representation is not specified by the publisher card or FAQ.

The oracle should cover two independently installed walls and ordinary piece movement, captures, attacks, Pawn double steps, and en passant. Exclude castling, swaps, placements, card jumps, and other movement cards from this scope. No initial-mate or after-move rescue claim is made by this source note.
