Man-Trap / Truce source verdict

No explicit publisher ruling for this exact pairing was found. An opposing entrant cannot be captured during an active Truce. Whether its entry nevertheless consumes the trap is not expressly settled by the supplied primary sources. Retaining the trap is the stronger inference from the publisher's capture-immunity explanation and the local rule's “next unprotected opposing piece” wording, but must not be represented as an explicit official Truce ruling.

Evidence (all paths below relative to the repository root):

Man-Trap is absent from that list, and no consumption rule is given. Every Man-Trap and Truce occurrence in the supplied FAQ text was searched; no exact pairing was present.
